# Drills

Load **only** when the learner asked for drills or practice. Not first-contact teaching. Not flashcards, study guides, or other resource creation.

**Aim** (in order): this session's misses → open `Progress-context.md` items for this thread → Physics leftover slots: GRE-weighted topics for the area.

**Shape**
- Mixed judgment — no labeled sections.
- ≥1 misconception trap (canonical error or this learner's last slip).
- Physics: GRE difficulty (multi-step, two-way checks, invert formulas, traps). Not plug-ins. Not for English/TOEFL.
- No hints or answer keys in the session note until after they attempt, unless they ask.

**Turns (weak-spot loop)**
- Several short drill turns — not one dump, then done.
- After each attempt: mark misses and re-weight the next batch toward those weak spots.
- Close by naming **2–3 error-prone patterns** (how they keep going wrong), not a list of missed words. Then stop.
- While this loop is running, the SKILL.md one-question tutoring rhythm **yields** (batched items are the point).
