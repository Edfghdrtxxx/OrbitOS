**Trigger:** 创世级, genesis papers, STAGE 1 鸟瞰主线, or “groundbreaking paper” (weak alias).

创世级文章 = **genesis papers**: they created the ontology of the field. “Groundbreaking” is weaker English; do not treat it as the primary gloss.

1. **Mayer 1948** — On Closed Shells in Nuclei, *Phys. Rev.* **74**, 235. Compiled the empirical case that 20, 50, 82, and 126 are closed shells.
2. **Mayer 1949 II** — On Closed Shells in Nuclei. II, *Phys. Rev.* **75**, 1969. Introduced strong spin-orbit coupling that produces magic numbers 28, 50, 82, 126.
3. **Bohr–Mottelson 1953** — Collective and individual-particle aspects of nuclear structure, *Dan. Mat. Fys. Medd.* **27**, no. 16. Unified collective rotation/vibration with the individual-particle shell model.
4. **Nilsson 1955** — Binding states of individual nucleons in strongly deformed nuclei, *Dan. Mat. Fys. Medd.* **29**, no. 16. Computed deformed single-particle energy levels (Nilsson diagrams).
5. **Otsuka 2001** — Magic Numbers in Exotic Nuclei and Spin-Isospin Properties of the NN Interaction, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **87**, 082502. Showed exotic magic numbers migrate via the spin-isospin NN interaction.
