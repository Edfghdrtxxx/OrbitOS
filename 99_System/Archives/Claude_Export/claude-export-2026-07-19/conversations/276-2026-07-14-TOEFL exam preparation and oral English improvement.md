Frame: *"Last [spring] I tried [specific experiment] for [a month]: I [did A], [did B], and [did C]. By the [third week] I was [measurable change]."*
The list of three concrete actions plus a timestamped result is what makes an opinion sound lived rather than invented. Note the plain verbs — cooked, walked, read — not impressive vocabulary.

**5. The reframe wrap-up.**
Frame: *"So [X] isn't [the negative framing] — it's [your positive reframe]."* / *"It didn't just [obvious benefit]; it [deeper benefit]."*
Both versions close by adding a final idea instead of merely restating, which is what pushes Topic Development up in the last five seconds.

Frame: *"My rule is simple: [one-sentence principle]."*
This one doubles as an emergency tool — if you feel a restart loop coming mid-answer (your "first of all first of all" pattern), this frame gives you a short, grammatically closed sentence to land on and reset from.
