Here's a rundown of popular focus apps, grouped by what they're best at:

**Distraction/website blockers**
- **Freedom** – blocks distracting sites and apps across Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and Chrome, with a "Locked Mode" that prevents you from disabling the block once a session starts.
- **Cold Turkey / SelfControl** – strict, hard-to-bypass blockers; SelfControl is free and open-source for Mac, and can't be turned off until its timer runs out.
- **LeechBlock NG / StayFocusd** – browser-extension blockers with custom rules and daily time limits.
- **one sec** – adds a short intentional delay before you can open a distracting app.

**Pomodoro / timer-based**
- **Forest** – grows a virtual tree while you stay off your phone; a low-stakes, free option that's especially popular for phone-addiction and impulse control.
- **Session Pomodoro** and **Focus To-Do** – combine Pomodoro timers with task management.

**Planning / task-focused**
- **Week Plan** – built around weekly priorities rather than raw to-do volume, emphasizing a goal-first approach that aligns daily work with longer-term objectives.
- **Sunsama** – guided daily planning and timeboxing.
- **Reclaim.ai** – auto-schedules focus time around your calendar and meetings.

**ADHD-oriented**
- **Lifestack** – schedules your day around energy levels rather than just the clock.
- **Goblin Tools** – breaks overwhelming tasks into smaller steps.
- **Brain.fm** – functional audio designed to extend focus sessions.

**Usage tracking**
- **RescueTime / StayFree** – detailed analytics on where your screen time actually goes.

**Ambient/body-doubling**
- **Flown** – virtual co-working sessions where you work silently alongside others to boost focus and accountability.
- **Endel** – focus/sleep soundscapes.
