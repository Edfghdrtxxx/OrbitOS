In PowerPoint, you can find the **Slide Master** by going to the **View** tab on the ribbon, then clicking **Slide Master** in the Master Views group.

From there you can edit the master slides, which control the overall layout, fonts, colors, and background for your presentation. When you're done editing, click **Close Master View** to return to your normal slides.

If you're looking for the **Handout Master** or **Notes Master**, those are also in the same Master Views group under the View tab.

Great, you're already in the Slide Master view! To make new slides use that specific layout, here's what to do:

**Step 1:** Click **Close Master View** (the button with the red X on the right side of the ribbon) to go back to normal editing view.

**Step 2:** When you want to insert a new slide, don't just click the top half of the "New Slide" button (that inserts the default layout). Instead, click the **bottom half** (the small dropdown arrow) of the **New Slide** button on the Home tab.

**Step 3:** A gallery of all available layouts will appear — find and click the one you just edited (it should show the blue header with the CAS logo and "Edit the layout" title area). PowerPoint will name it based on the layout name you see in the left panel.

**Tip to make it the default:** If you want *every* new slide to automatically use that layout, you can right-click that layout in the New Slide dropdown gallery and choose **Set as Default Layout**. After that, simply clicking the top half of the New Slide button will use your preferred layout.

You can also **rename** the layout to something recognizable (like "IMP Standard") by right-clicking on it in the Slide Master view panel and choosing **Rename Layout** — that makes it easier to find later.
