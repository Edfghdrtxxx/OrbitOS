If you're hungry in the evening but want something gentle on your stomach, here are some good options:

**Light and soothing choices:** A small bowl of oatmeal or congee (粥) is one of the easiest things to digest — warm, soft, and not too heavy before bed. Plain rice with a light broth works similarly well.

**Protein that won't weigh you down:** A soft-boiled or steamed egg, a small portion of steamed fish, or some plain yogurt are all relatively easy on digestion compared to red meat or fried foods.

**Fruits:** Bananas are particularly gentle on the stomach and mildly filling. Cooked or stewed apples are another good option. Avoid citrus or very acidic fruits late at night, as they can cause reflux.

**Simple snacks:** A small handful of nuts (not too many — they're calorie-dense), some warm milk, or a few plain crackers can take the edge off hunger without overloading your digestive system.

A few things worth avoiding in the evening: spicy or greasy food, raw vegetables in large quantities (harder to break down), and heavy carb-loaded meals — these all take longer to digest and can disrupt sleep.

The general rule of thumb is to keep it warm, soft, and small in portion if you're eating within a couple hours of sleeping.
