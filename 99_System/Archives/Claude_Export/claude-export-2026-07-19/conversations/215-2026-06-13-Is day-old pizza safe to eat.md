### 2. assistant — 2026-06-13T03:16:08.738939Z

Pizza left out overnight at room temperature isn't safe to eat. The USDA's guidance is that perishable food left at room temperature for more than 2 hours should be discarded, because bacteria multiply rapidly between 40°F and 140°F (the "danger zone"). From last evening to today's noon is far past that window.

If the pizza was refrigerated within ~2 hours of being bought, it's fine — refrigerated pizza is good for 3–4 days. Reheat it to steaming hot (165°F).

So the key question is: was it left out on the counter, or put in the fridge? Out on the counter overnight → toss it. In the fridge → reheat and enjoy.
