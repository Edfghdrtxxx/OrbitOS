**Target Intake:** Doctoral Program (D1), October 2027 Enrollment  
**Application Window:** Period 2: December 7 – December 22, 2026 (JST)  
**Prospective Supervisor:** Prof. Nobuaki Imai (Center for Nuclear Study [CNS], DONUTS Group, RIKEN Wako Campus; Sub-course A2)  
**Applicant Context:** Master's in Particle & Nuclear Physics (IMP/CAS, UCAS degree expected June 2027); TOEFL iBT 100/120 (C1, 2026-08-30); GRE Physics booked 2026-11-01  
**Document Purpose:** Definitive logistics mapping for the Special Selection interview, screening mechanics, timeline, format, and visa/travel requirements.

## 1. Hard Requirements (Must-Do)

1. **Selection Track [VERIFIED / PROFESSOR]:**
   - Must apply through **Special Selection for International Applicants (外国人特別選考, SSIA)**.
   - **NOT GSGC [NOT THIS TRACK]:** GSGC is a 5-year integrated MSc+PhD track requiring master's entry; applicants who already hold or will hold a master's degree are ineligible for D1 direct entry (confirmed by Prof. Imai on 2026-04-13).
   - **NOT Regular Entrance Examination (一般入試) [NOT THIS TRACK]:** General entrance examination requires written exams in August in Tokyo and is a separate domestic track.
2. **Pre-Consultation & Single Supervisor Rule [VERIFIED / PROFESSOR]:**
   - Department of Physics strictly mandates choosing **only ONE potential supervising professor** (unlike other departments allowing up to 3).
   - Must obtain consent to supervise prior to applying and submit documentary proof (email correspondence copy). No Preliminary Evaluation Certificate (PEC) required for Physics.
   - Prof. Nobuaki Imai confirmed sole supervision commitment on 2026-04-13.
3. **No Written Examination [PROFESSOR / VERIFIED]:**
   - Department of Physics Special Selection consists of **document-based review and interview only**; there is **no written examination** (confirmed by Prof. Imai 2026-04-13).
   - Contrast: GSGC requires a 2-hour online written examination (administered mid-January) [NOT THIS TRACK].
4. **Standardized Test Gates [VERIFIED / PROFESSOR]:**
   - **GRE Physics Subject Test:** Required for Physics (School of Science code: **7048**). GRE General is **not** required. Competitive practical minimum is **900** per Prof. Imai (2026-04-13). Score must be from a test taken within 2 years of the application start date.
   - **TOEFL iBT:** Required (School of Science code: **8002-01**). Must be from a single test date; MyBest scores are rejected. Applicant locked score: 100/120 (C1, test date 2026-08-30).
5. **Interview Screening Gate — Not Automatic for All Applicants [VERIFIED / INFERENCE]:**
   - An interview is **not guaranteed for every applicant**.
   - Official guidelines state: *"Successful applicants ... will be selected based on an evaluation of their applications, submitted documents and test scores. Some Departments may have additional requirements such as interviews..."* (GSS Guidelines Section 2).
   - Sibling department EPS explicitly states: *"Applicants who pass the documentary assessment will be invited by the Department to take an online examination."*
   - Therefore, an interview invitation is contingent upon passing the initial document screening and score threshold (GRE Physics ≥ 900 benchmark).

### Modality Justification & Contrast
- **Online modality confirmed across all international tracks:**
  1. Earth and Planetary Science (EPS) Special Selection explicitly states: *"The online examination will be held using Zoom. There will be a 'connection test day' before the test date"* (fetched 2026-09-09).
  2. Department of Astronomy explicitly specifies: *"online interviews may be conducted if necessary"* (fetched 2026-09-09).
  3. Department of Physics itself conducts its Regular Doctoral Entrance Examination oral exam online: *"口述試験 2027年1月26日（火）予定 オンラインで実施します"* (`phys.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/g_info/49259/`).
  4. GSGC written and oral examinations are administered remotely (`phys.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/admission/462/`).
- **No in-person travel to Japan is required for the examination.**

### Visa & Travel Implications
- **No Short-Term Visa Needed for Exam:** Since the interview is conducted online, the candidate does not need to apply for a short-term visitor visa (短期滞在ビザ) or book travel to Japan during January/February 2027.
- **Post-Admission Student Visa (留学ビザ):**
  - Upon notification of acceptance in early March 2027, the UTokyo Graduate School of Science International Liaison Office (ILO) contacts successful applicants regarding visa processing.
  - Certificate of Eligibility (COE, 在留資格認定証明書) application is prepared between May and July 2027.
  - COE is typically issued in July/August 2027; applicant applies for the College Student Visa at the Japanese Embassy/Consulate in Beijing before departure for October 1, 2027 enrollment.

## 3. Typical Content / Questions

Based on Graduate School of Science doctoral oral examinations and experimental nuclear physics expectations:

1. **Master's Thesis Presentation & Methodology (~15–20 min):**
   - Detailed walkthrough of active-target TPC (MATE-TPC) research, detector physics, signal processing, and drift chamber tracking.
   - Deep dive into Machine Learning methodologies (ResNet, Vision Transformers, domain adaptation / unsupervised transfer learning for particle identification).
   - Experimental validation, beam tests, efficiency, resolution, and physics conclusions.
2. **Doctoral Research Proposal & Alignment (~5–10 min):**
   - Articulation of research plan under Prof. Imai: low-energy direct nuclear reactions utilizing the OEDO decelerator and SHARAQ spectrometer at RIKEN RIBF.
   - The SAKURA surrogate $(d,p)$ reaction campaign and its role in constraining neutron-capture $(n,\gamma)$ cross-sections for r-process astrophysical nucleosynthesis.
   - Transferability of applicant's detector instrumentation and advanced PID/data analysis skillset to the DONUTS group experimental program.
3. **Technical Grilling & Foundational Physics Q&A (~10–15 min):**
   - Fundamental quantum mechanics and nuclear reaction theory (optical potential, DWBA, transfer reactions, resonances).
   - Handling of systematic uncertainties, background suppression, detector calibration, and phase-space kinematics.
   - General physics competence (grounded by the GRE Physics 900+ target).
4. **Logistics, Funding & Motivation (~5 min):**
   - Confirmation of master's degree graduation timeline (June 2027 from IMP/UCAS).
   - Awareness of financial plans (RIKEN JRA application, doctoral research assistantships, self-renting in Wako city as confirmed by Prof. Imai).
