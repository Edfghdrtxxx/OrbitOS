# Registers

Audited phrasing, all surfaces in one file. Guidelines stay short; examples carry the standard.

## Slide asides

Natural prose beside a formula or figure (concept layer when a symbol gloss is not enough).

- Define by identity: formation chain, condition, or measurable property.
- One idea per line; about 1–3 tight sentences.
- Contrast with a positive comparative (`slower than the beam`, `at \(v_{\mathrm{cm}}\) rather than beam velocity`), or leave contrast to lead/cards/figure.
- Prefer lineage nouns (*remnant*, *evaporation residue*, *recoil*) over bare *product*.
- For energy symbols: the symbol **is** the quantity under a stated condition — it does not “compare” two unrelated stories.

### Examples

**Velocity Filters — residue / \(E_r\)**

- **Residue:** heavy remnant of the compound nucleus after it evaporates a few light particles.
- **\(E_r = (A_p/A_c) E_p\):** the residue’s kinetic energy under full momentum transfer: motion at \(v_{\mathrm{cm}}\), slower than the beam.

**Velocity Filters — factor 3 in Eq. 5**

- **Factor 3:** each emission kicks the residue in a random direction. The squared kicks add up, and x, y and z share the sum equally; \(\sigma_r\) is the spread along one axis, so \(\sigma_r^2\) gets a third.

## Outline questions

The audience's own questions on outline slides.

- Name the exact cost; do not overstate it.
- Keep the question open; no verdict words (*superior*, *better*).

### Examples

**Velocity Filters — the mass trade-off**

- ✗ *Why give up measuring mass?* — sounds as if mass is never known.
- ✓ *The filter does not measure mass in flight.* — names the one step skipped; mass is recovered later by decay spectroscopy.

## Chinese self-media copy

News-style posts and short-video narration in Chinese. The only home of these rules: `curiosity-video` `AGENTS.md` rule 3 points here.

**Wording — posts and narration**

- Keep life-or-death words (*生死*, *背水一战*, *殊死一搏*, *至暗时刻*, *刺刀见红*) for events where the source states that survival is at risk. A promotion, launch, or revision takes the plain action noun.
- Drop a modifier that repeats what the noun already says: *冲刺* already means a short, urgent effort.
- When the sentence carries a quoted rule, number, or other concrete detail, that detail supplies the force; no intensifier beside it.
- State the basis of a comparison before the numbers: which price (API or subscription) and which tier.
- Check an ordinal word (*率先*, *最先*, *首次*) against the timeline; when an earlier event exists, tell it first.
- Explain a term at first use in one short sentence; a written post may use one bracket.
- Gloss a platform mechanism literally, then give one analogy from the reader's own experience.
- State what is uncertain in plain words; no softening chain and no prediction.
- Name the specific areas where a product leads, as the author observed them.
- Keep the author's own sayings (*羊毛出在羊身上*).

**Written news comment — posts only**

Video narration takes its structure and spoken style from the 讲法 rows of `planning/pipeline-playbook.md` section 5 in `curiosity-video`.

- Order a news comment as: event, each cause of pressure, result, responses in time order, what the response shows, own view.
- One point per paragraph. Open the paragraph with a short sentence that names the point.
- Put the date or number in brackets directly after the name it belongs to.
- State the verdict in first person in its own paragraph, apart from the report. Follow it with reasons, each resting on a fact.

### Examples

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — *生死冲刺* reduced to *冲刺***

- OpenAI 开启 28 天“不改进就送额度”的冲刺

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — comparison basis**

- 如果从 API 的角度来看，Astra 的价格是 Opus 的两倍以上、Sonnet 的四倍以上，而 OpenAI 同等级的订阅额度本来就比 Claude 少。

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — Full Reset analogy**

- 这可以类比游戏里的体力机制：体力原本要隔一周才会恢复到 100%，一次 Full Reset 直接回满，不用再等一周。

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — paragraph openers**

- 价格是另一个原因。
- 面对压力，OpenAI 先在模型上回应：
- 接着是 Codex 团队。

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — what the response shows**

- 拿“要么交付功能，要么补偿额度”做公开承诺，说明大模型的竞争已经不只比模型参数，也在比开发者留存和工程交付速度。

**OpenAI 28-day campaign — verdict and reasons**

- 但我个人并不看好 OpenAI。
- 一家算力紧张的公司，用额度做补偿，能补多少并不确定。
- 并且，羊毛出在羊身上。前段时间大规模 reset 之后，用户的额度明显缩水。
