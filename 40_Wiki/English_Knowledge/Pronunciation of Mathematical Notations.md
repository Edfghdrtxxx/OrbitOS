# Pronunciation of Mathematical Notations

> [!important] All math expressions, equations, and formulas **must** use LaTeX notation (`$...$` inline, `$$...$$` display). Prefer the canonical academic / textbook form (full PDG-style expression, conventional symbols, standard correction terms) over simplified or pedagogical variants.

## Definition

Standardized spoken forms of mathematical, algebraic, and physical symbols in English. Converting symbolic expressions into natural English requires treating relational operators as finite verbs, exponents and fractions as specific syntactic templates, and diacritics as post-positive modifiers to avoid calquing from native language patterns.

## Key Points

- **Relations as verbs:** Relational symbols function as finite verbs in a sentence. For example, $\le$ is read as *"is less than or equal to"* (never drop *"than"* or *"to"*), and $=$ is read as *"equals"* or *"is equal to"*.
- **Diacritics and accents:** In physics and calculus, diacritical marks are spoken immediately after the letter: $\hat{r}$ is *"r-hat"*, $\dot{r}$ is *"r-dot"*, $\ddot{r}$ is *"r-double-dot"*, $\bar{x}$ is *"x-bar"*, and $r'$ is *"r-prime"* (US) or *"r-dash"* (UK).
- **Fractions and division:** During fluid derivations, $\frac{a}{b}$ is conventionally spoken as *"$a$ over $b$"* rather than *"a divided by b"* or *"the fraction of a and b"*.
- **Function arguments:** $f(x)$ is spoken as *"$f$ of $x$"*, never *"$f$ bracket $x$"*.
- **Subscripts and powers:** $x_i$ is *"$x$ sub $i$"* (or simply *"$x$ $i$"*), while $x^2$ is *"$x$ squared"*, $x^3$ is *"$x$ cubed"*, and $x^n$ is *"$x$ to the $n$th"* or *"$x$ to the power of $n$"*.

## Examples

### 1. Relations and Inequalities
| Notation | LaTeX | Standard Spoken English | Contextual / Conversational |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\le$ | `\le`, `\leq` | *"is less than or equal to"* | *"at most"*, *"no more than"* |
| $\ge$ | `\ge`, `\geq` | *"is greater than or equal to"* | *"at least"*, *"no less than"* |
| $<$ | `<` | *"is less than"* | *"strictly less than"* |
| $>$ | `>` | *"is greater than"* | *"strictly greater than"* |
| $=$ | `=` | *"equals"*, *"is equal to"* | — |
| $\neq$ | `\neq` | *"is not equal to"*, *"does not equal"* | — |
| $\approx$ | `\approx` | *"is approximately equal to"* | *"is roughly"*, *"is about"* |
| $\ll$, $\gg$ | `\ll`, `\gg` | *"is much less than"*, *"is much greater than"* | — |
| $\sim$ | `\sim` | *"is on the order of"* / *"scales as"* | *"is distributed as"* (statistics) |
| $\propto$ | `\propto` | *"is proportional to"* | — |
| $\equiv$ | `\equiv` | *"is identically equal to"*, *"is defined as"* | *"is congruent to"* (modular arithmetic) |

### 2. Physics Modifiers & Derivatives
| Notation | LaTeX | Standard Spoken English | Field Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\hat{r}$ | `\hat{r}` | *"r-hat"* | Unit vector in the direction of $\vec{r}$ |
| $\dot{r}, \ddot{r}$ | `\dot{r}, \ddot{r}` | *"r-dot"*, *"r-double-dot"* | Time derivative $\frac{dr}{dt}, \frac{d^2r}{dt^2}$ |
| $r'$ | `r'` | *"r-prime"* (US), *"r-dash"* (UK) | Often source coordinate in potential integrals |
| $\bar{x}$ | `\bar{x}` | *"x-bar"* | Mean / average or anti-particle |
| $\vec{r}$ | `\vec{r}` | *"vector r"*, *"r vector"* | Vector notation |
| $\nabla \cdot \vec{B}$ | `\nabla \cdot \vec{B}` | *"div B"*, *"divergence of B"* | In full: *"del dot B"* |
| $\nabla \times \vec{E}$ | `\nabla \times \vec{E}` | *"curl E"*, *"curl of E"* | In full: *"del cross E"* |
| $\frac{df}{dx}$ | `\frac{df}{dx}` | *"d f by d x"*, *"d f d x"* | *"derivative of f with respect to x"* |
| $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$ | `\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}` | *"partial f by partial x"*, *"del f by del x"* | Partial derivative |

### 3. Common Operations & Calculus
| Notation | LaTeX | Standard Spoken English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $a \pm b$ | `a \pm b` | *"$a$ plus or minus $b$"* |
| $\frac{a}{b}$ | `\frac{a}{b}` | *"$a$ over $b$"* (informal), *"$a$ divided by $b$"* (formal) |
| $x^2, x^3, x^n$ | `x^2, x^3, x^n` | *"$x$ squared"*, *"$x$ cubed"*, *"$x$ to the $n$"* |
| $\sqrt{x}, \sqrt[n]{x}$ | `\sqrt{x}, \sqrt[n]{x}` | *"square root of $x$"*, *"nth root of $x$"* |
| $|x|$ | `|x|` | *"absolute value of $x$"*, *"mod $x$"*, *"magnitude of $x$"* |
| $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ | `\int_a^b f(x)\,dx` | *"the integral from $a$ to $b$ of $f$ of $x$ $d x$"* |
| $\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$ | `\sum_{i=1}^n x_i` | *"the sum from $i$ equals one to $n$ of $x$ sub $i$"* |
| $\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)$ | `\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)` | *"the limit of $f$ of $x$ as $x$ approaches zero"* |
