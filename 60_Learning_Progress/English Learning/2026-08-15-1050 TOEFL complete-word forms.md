### Turn 11 — 2026-08-15 (learner)

> The noun nature one. Gerund is not valid behind an adjective.

### Tutor

*Plagiarism* — yes.

The gerund rule is a little too absolute. English does allow adjective + *-ing* sometimes (*the accidental killing*).
