## Known solid

### TOEFL Academic Discussion

Session: [[2026-07-26-2233 TOEFL Academic Discussion]]

- **One-claim opening:** Names a single impact that answers the professor (not a list).
- **Why = stuck $\to$ tool $\to$ keep going:** Draft a mini-example chain for "learning momentum".
- **No-filler close:** Verb-driven restatement of claim; cut stacked hedges and noun piles on second attempt.
- **Kill inflation vs effective complexity:** "I firmly align with the perspective that" $\to$ "I believe"; rule: if deleting the phrase leaves meaning intact, it is padding.
- **Stance opening:** "I share [Name]'s perspective that..." takes `that + clause` (not `regarding + noun phrase`); noun `perspective` (not adverb).
- **Transitions:** `First... Furthermore` for supporting reasons; avoid `On the other hand` unless contrasting two distinct sides.
- **Mechanism verbs vs noun piles:** Concrete actions (*pinpoint logical gaps*, *narrow down a research question*) replace empty abstractions (*supply essential resources*, *study quality*).
- **Reason clause:** "The reason... is that" (not "is because", which introduces double cause).
- **Relative clause agreement:** Check noun directly before `which/that` (singular `feedback/environment` $\to$ singular verb; plural $\to$ plural).
- **Uncountable nouns:** *feedback*, *information*, *advice*, *research* have no plural form.
- **Less vs fewer:** *fewer* for countable nouns; *less* for uncountable.

### TOEFL listening paraphrase

Session: [[2026-08-27-2255 TOEFL distinct outcomes paraphrase]]

- **Too broad trap:** Mixed category, not long survey (long survey + only depression $\to$ Park would not object).
- **Noun overlap trap:** Noun overlap $\neq$ correct option; reject "well focused" + four passage nouns when judgment is wrong.
- **Keep if judgment matches:** Decode meaning before matching words; avoid spurious AND joins.
- **Outcome definition:** *outcome* = thing the study measures (distinct from *objective* = aim/hypothesis).

### TOEFL detail & EXCEPT traps (General vs. Specific)

Session: [[2026-09-02-1400 TOEFL general vs specific details]]

- **One-Way Street (Valid):** Specific (text) $\to$ General (option) is a valid generalization (e.g. *Buddhism* $\to$ *religious ideas*).
- **One-Way Street (Trap):** General (text) $\to$ Specific (option) is an invalid over-specification trap (e.g. *technology* $\to$ *transportation technology*).
- **Unsupported modifiers:** Options adding unmentioned qualifiers (*new*, *mass-produced*, specific sub-types) to general passage nouns are invalid.

### Relative words

Session: [[2026-08-03-1059 that-which-where]]

- **Identify vs extra:** Restrictive (*that*, no commas) vs non-restrictive (*which*, with commas).
- **No resumptive pronoun:** *that is*, never *that it is*.
- **Where vs in which:** *where* $\approx$ *in/at which* for spatial places.
- **Subject relative:** *that* as subject of relative verb for inanimate things (*method that reduces...*, not *where reduces*).
- **Person relative:** *who* for person + non-restrictive + subject (*Dr. Imai, who supervises...*).
- **Object relative:** *that* as object (*screens that cannot replicate [skills]*).
- **Formal object:** *whom* / *with whom*.
- **Possession:** *whose* for possession.
- **Relative that vs conjunction that:** *argue that...* is a conjunction clause, not a relative clause.

- **Mid-position manner / finally:** Placed after modal auxiliary (if present), before main verb.

### Collocations and noun logic

Sessions: [[2026-08-11-1805 collocations and noun logic]] · [[2026-08-15-1050 TOEFL complete-word forms]] · [[2026-08-15-1435 gerunds]]

- **Phrasal verbs:** *take place* functions as "happen"; event is the grammatical subject.
- **Spatial logic:** Navigate *from* a "location" (abstract coordinate) *to* a "building" (physical structure).
- **Compound pairing:** Lock target object first (*backup copies* $\to$ *cloud folder*; *lab manuals* $\to$ *chemistry course*).
- **Frozen compound $\neq$ 的-reshuffle:** English campus noun chunks are fixed units; Chinese modifier reshuffle (*course manuals* for *lab manuals*) is an error.
- **Question anchor over decoy PP:** The office/person in question anchors the noun (housing office $\to$ *roommate problem*, not hallway-*printer*).
- **Compound container + event:** [container] + [activity/event] (*seminar discussion* $\to$ venue container + head event).
- **Modifier typing:** *main claim* / *central thesis* takes hierarchical qualifiers; *textual evidence* / *empirical data* takes source/grounding qualifiers.
- **Search vs research results:** *search results* = hit-list narrowed by filters; *research results* = empirical findings of an investigation.
- **Complete-word strategy:** Determine grammatical slot function before guessing from letter stems.
- **Remarkably + adjective:** Adverb modifies adjective (*remarkably innovative*), not bare noun.
- **Plagiarism definition & forms:** Dedicated noun *plagiarism*; verb *plagiarize* (*prevent accidental plagiarism* $\to$ noun; *plagiaring* is invalid).
- **Gerund as activity name:** Gerund functions as noun/object chair (*I enjoy swimming* swaps with *music*); activity feel $\neq$ sentence verb.
- **Gerund after preposition:** *interested in learning* / *good at swimming* (object of preposition, never bare base form).
- **Participle as adjective:** *The swimming child* (describer, not sentence predicate).
- **Verb catenation:** Verbs hire specific complements (*enjoy swimming* vs *want to swim*).
- **Stop + ing vs to:** *stop smoking* = quit habit; *stop to smoke* = pause another activity in order to smoke.
- **Remember + ing vs to:** *remember doing* = memory of completed past action; *remember to do* = obligation preceding action.
- **Collocations:** *consent procedures* (clear participant permission in fieldwork ethics).
- **Transform into vs transfer to:** $A \to B$ nature/identity change takes *into*; physical/ownership relocation takes *to*.

### Articles (TOEFL options)

Session: [[2026-08-12-1615 articles in TOEFL responses]]

- **Count nouns after prepositions:** Bare singular count noun requires article (*at the bookstore*; prefer *reception desk* in AmE).
- **Subject-verb agreement:** Plural subject requires base verb (*people post*, not *posts*).

### Causal conjunctions (since / as / because)

Session: [[2026-08-14-1618 since-as-because]]

- **Scope boundaries:** *since* $\not\subset$ *as*; *since* carries temporal origin (*since 2020*), *as 2020* is invalid.
- **As + noun:** Denotes role (*As a physicist...*; *since a student* is invalid).
- **Why-test (given vs new):** *because* introduces new explanatory information; fronted *since* introduces given/shared premises.
- **As + clause:** Differentiates simultaneous time (*while*) vs causal reason (*because*).

### Prepositions (at / on / in time)

Session: [[2026-08-19-1555 prepositions]]

- **Time scope dimensions:** *at* = point in time (3 p.m.); *on* = day as flat surface (*on Monday*, *on December 25*); *in* = container/volume (*in 2020*, *in December*).
- **Preposition selection by verb:** Verbs select their governing prepositions (*thank for*, *congratulate on*).
- **Topic vs field:** *research on* a specific topic vs *research in* a broad disciplinary field.
- **Parts of day:** *in the morning*, *at night*; named day overrides and drops article: *on Friday morning*, *on Monday night*.

### along / alongside

Session: [[2026-08-25-1451 along-alongside]]

- **along with:** Secondary addition accompanying the primary subject ("extra in the bag").
- **alongside:** Parallel side-by-side neighbor (spatial or conceptual alignment).
- **along:** Traversal along the continuous linear length of an object (*along the beamline*).

### Academic email requests and collocations

Session: [[2026-08-26-1505 academic email requests]]

- **Request verbs:** *approve* / *grant* / *accommodate* a request (*consent a request* is invalid).
- **Polite conditionals:** *appreciate it if...* (dummy pronoun *it* required) vs *be grateful if...* (adjective predicate, no *it*).
- **Conflict idioms:** *a scheduling conflict has arisen* (present perfect collocation).
- **Prerequisite vs indispensable:** *prerequisite* for mandatory formal conditions; *indispensable* for vital contributors/tools.

### beyond

Session: [[2026-08-27-1012 beyond]]

- **Past vs beyond:** *past* = traveler viewpoint traversing along a trajectory; *beyond* = boundary viewpoint looking across a threshold into external domain.
- **Abstract domains:** *beyond repair* = outside the operational domain of fixability.
- **Besides vs beyond:** *besides* = additive inclusion ($A + B$); *beyond* = surpassing a reference baseline or stepping to a higher tier.

### appeal / appeal to

Session: [[2026-08-27-1641 appeal]]

- **Subject selection:** Person-subject $\to$ formal request/petition; thing-subject $\to$ attraction/interest.
- **Prepositional frame:** *appeal to* [person/body] *for* [goal] (*appeal to the committee for beam time*).
- **Predicate syntax:** *The idea appeals to me* (active verb, never *attracts to*).
- **Intransitive legal/academic:** *decided to appeal* = formal procedural challenge.

### TOEFL campus communication & response selection

Sessions: [[2026-08-29-2046 TOEFL best campus reply]] · [[2026-09-01-1030 TOEFL listening choose best response]] · [[2026-08-29-1018 TOEFL main purpose]]

- **Reply ranking:** Comprehensive stack (multiple legitimate administrative channels) beats single-door responses.
- **Alternative questions ($A \text{ or } B$):** Rejects bare *Yes/No*; requires specific choice or indifference expression (*Whatever is more convenient*).
- **Presupposition defeat:** Address or challenge underlying premise (*He usually takes the bus*) rather than irrelevant subject displacement.
- **Event vs routine:** Specific-event prompt (*tomorrow*) requires situational explanation rather than frequency statement (*twice a week*).
- **Institutional register:** Transactional service actions (*take reservations*) are restricted to service desk roles, not peer conversation.
- **Strip-out test:** Administrative notices can drop auxiliary reminder stickers; the core speech-act is the package.
- **Package = one job:** Speech-act of the mail; auxiliary reminder stickers or conditions can drop without breaking message integrity.
- **Confirm-family bundle:** *[whose] + [booked entity] + [lock-in verb]* (*your reservation is confirmed*).
- **Job-to-job gate:** Same words + wrong communicative job $\to$ reject; different words + same communicative job $\to$ accept (paraphrase gate).

### would rather ... than

Session: [[2026-09-03-0819 would rather than]]

- **Parallel bare verbs:** *would rather [do A] than [do B]* (strips *-ing* and *to* from both sides; modal *would* governs both).
- **Ellipsis:** Omit repeated verb across *than* (*eat Italian food than Japanese food*).
- **Prefer vs would rather:** *prefer [A] to [B]* (hires *to* + gerunds/nouns); *would rather [A] than [B]* (hires *than* + bare verbs).
- **Prefer infinitive:** *prefer to [do A] rather than [(do) B]* (infinitives with *prefer* hire *rather than*, not *to* or bare *than*).
- **Preposition retention:** *talk to Alex than to Maria* (*to* retained to disambiguate object vs subject).
- **Negation syntax:** *would rather not [verb]* (*not* placed immediately after *rather*; never *wouldn't rather*).
