## 3. Does the 0.90 claim survive?

**As stated, no.** "max|h| flags unseen carbon isotopes at AUROC ≈ 0.90" is an artifact of the negative set: with negatives = seen clutter only, the seen proton channel scores the same 0.90. The honest restatement supported by local evidence:

> A sparse penultimate unit separates the heavy elastic-recoil channels (and the proton target channel) from the light-fragment channels at AUROC ≈ 0.90, but does not separate unseen from seen events (pooled unseen vs all seen: 0.67/0.58) nor the unseen isotopes from each other (≈0.5).

**Proxy vs learned structure: unresolved locally.** The channel ordering, single-unit sparsity, and F/G/H degeneracy are all consistent with a trivial observable (total deposited charge, track extent, or the stored `total_mass` physics feature). The moderate cross-arm per-event correlation (0.69) and class-conditional within-channel correlations leave room for learned structure on top. Only the observable join (§5) decides; no local data can.
