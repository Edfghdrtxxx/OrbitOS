# UTokyo Special Selection Interview

> [!info] Scope
> Physics doctoral **Special Selection for International Applicants** (外国人特別選考), October 2027 enrollment, Period 2. Supervisor: [[Prof_Nobuaki_Imai|Nobuaki Imai]] (DONUTS / CNS). Compiled 2026-09-09 from GSS 募集要項 + Physics dept pages + Imai 2026-04-13. Not a project plan.

> [!success] Bottom line
> Official Physics Special Selection is **document + score review**. Interview is **department-optional** on the 要項; Physics has **published no interview protocol** (duration, date, Zoom vs campus, slides, committee). Imai: **interview only, no written exam**; GRE Physics practical min **900**. Do not import Regular 一般入試 oral or GSGC written-exam format into this track.

Claim flags in this note: **VERIFIED** (primary URL, fetched 2026-09-09) · **PROFESSOR** (Imai 2026-04-13) · **ANALOG** (other GSS dept / other exam) · **INFERENCE**.

## 2. What is actually required

From GSS 募集要項 EN/JA ([EN](https://www.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/admission/graduate.html) · [JA](https://www.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ja/admission/graduate.html)), **VERIFIED**:

**Selection method.** “Successful applicants … will be selected based on an evaluation of their applications, submitted documents and test scores.” JA: 応募書類に基づいて審査し入学者を決定する.

**Interview is not universal.** “Some Departments may have additional requirements such as interviews and online written examinations.” JA: 専攻によっては、面接試験やオンラインでの筆記試験を行うこともある. Physics §9 is only a link to the Physics homepage — **no Physics interview footnote**.

**Physics document package**

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| GRE Physics Subject | Required. General **not** required. Code **7048**. Within 2 years of application start. |
| TOEFL iBT | Test Date only (no MyBest). Code **8002-01**. Locked score: 100 (2026-08-30). |
| Official minima | **Unpublished.** FAQ: “We do not officially release any information regarding minimum score requirements for TOEFL/GRE.” |
| Supervisor | Physics: **exactly one**. Email proof of prior consultation. Consent ≠ admission. |
| Personal Statement | A4, 1–2 pp, English, single space — past study/research/initiative. |
| Statement of Purpose | A4, 1–2 pp, English — research to do at GSS, why this field, plan if possible, **Imai**. |
| Doctoral extras | Master's transcript + **thesis and/or publications**. Degree expected Jun 2027 → submit draft / papers + English abstract in Dec 2026. |
| Recommendations | Two, web-submitted by referees. |
| Fee | 10,000 JPY. |

**Imai (2026-04-13), PROFESSOR — not 募集要項**

- No written exam; interview only.
- GRE Physics practical competitive minimum **900**.
- Duration / content / date: unspecified.

GSS FAQ ([pdf](https://www.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/admission/pdf/faq.pdf)): no interview FAQ; Physics one-supervisor rule restated; IELTS not accepted.

## 6. Prep that is justified

### Must

- Own **PS + SoP + thesis/papers** in spoken English: methods, your role, failure modes, next measurement.
- One supervisor (Imai) + email trail. No PEC (that is EPS).
- GRE Physics as the published aptitude proxy; sit Nov 1 aiming **≥900**.
- Do **not** treat GSGC 2 h written or Regular August written as *this* exam.
- Calendar open **late Jan 2027** until Physics notifies.

### Analog drill (EPS / BioSci SS doctoral — not a promise)

- **15–20 min** master's talk (TPC, PID, ML, reaction physics) + **5 min** Imai-lab plan; practice **~40 min** with hostile Q&A.
- Alternate cut: **15 min** thesis + **10 min** “what I would do in DONUTS.”
- Zoom share of PDF/PPT, plus a no-slide 3 min backup.
- Drill **thesis-physics**, not a textbook tour: tracking, PID observables, ML leakage, what a wrong label does to a nuclear quantity.
- One page: why CNS / OEDO-SHARAQ rather than staying at IMP.

### Speculative — do not overfit

- GRE-style undergrad oral (QM/EM/stat mech). Low prior: Imai denied a written exam; Physics SS protocol unpublished.
- DONUTS syllabus (SAKURA, OEDO optics, ^{130}Sn surrogate). Useful as conversation, not a 要項.
- Japanese: Regular oral allows JP or EN; SS analogs are English-only. Japanese is **not** an admission requirement.

Imai/DONUTS pages are **lab descriptions, not exam lists**: [[Prof_Nobuaki_Imai]], [Imai Lab](https://www.cns.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/education/labs/imai/).

## 7. Quota note (do not misread)

Physics [専攻内規 (Nov 2020)](https://www.phys.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/numberofacceptance20201104.pdf) caps **master's** intake: 外国人特別選考 (incl. GSGC) **1 student per faculty per year** (exception: 2 if 専攻主任 approves, typically MEXT/GSGC). Title is 大学院**修士**課程. Doctoral SS quota is **not** stated as a hard 1/year rule there. Supplementary note mentions 外国人特別選考博士での受け入れ only as a case that can change master's-year counts. Do not treat “1 doctoral SS seat in Imai lab” as verified.
