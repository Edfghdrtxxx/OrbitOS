> [!info] Compiled 2026-09-11
> Survey of US employers in Tokyo for a **Chinese national** completing a **UTokyo Physics PhD** (~2031–32): experimental nuclear (TPC, direct reactions, PID) + ML (ResNet, ViT, DANN/MCD). Parent: [[Japan_Itinerary]] · immigration: [[Post_PhD_Immigration_Pathways]] · identity: [[Game_Framework]].

> [!warning] Not a pathway change

---

## Verdict

The claim “plenty of American companies in Tokyo for English speakers” is **true for SWE / some finance / sales**. It is **not** a nuclear-physics job market.

A physics PhD at a US firm in Tokyo is almost never “stay a nuclear physicist.” Firms buy: extract a weak signal from noisy data, write the model, defend the systematic, ship it. That maps to **four jobs**.

Google Japan ≠ US immigration. Chinese nationality is generally fine for IBM / Amazon / Microsoft DS / GS / Point72; **EAR / FEFTA** bites semiconductor-process and NVIDIA-export roles.

| # | Job | English-only? | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|

## Firm map

### Industrial research (tiny)

- **[IBM Research Tokyo](https://research.ibm.com/labs/japan)** (TRL, 1982; Hakozaki / Shin-Kawasaki / Kyoto) — only large US physics-hiring lab in Japan. FMs, quantum (UTokyo collaboration), semiconductors, analog AI. PhD/postdoc track.
- **MSR Asia Tokyo** (opened Nov 2024) — embodied AI, VLMs, vision, robotics. English lab language. Senior Researcher listed for Singapore/Tokyo. Tiny; needs vision/representation papers (ViT is a start).
- **Google Research Tokyo** — listed for ML / speech / NLP. Live Tokyo postings are mostly Cloud/GTM. Do not plan on a Research Scientist seat.
- **Schrödinger Tokyo** — computational materials; not experimental nuclear.

### Product ML

- Amazon Japan Applied Scientist (PhD typically **L5**).
- Microsoft Japan Data Scientist (Sugiura path).
- Google Tokyo applied ML / customer engineering — not core RS.

### Quant / Strats (Tokyo exists; volume is HK/SG)

**Open in Tokyo:**
- **Point72** — [Tokyo since 2011](https://point72.com/locations/japan-office/), Otemachi One Tower (2025). Cubist = systematic. Academy: 50%+ non-finance majors.
- **Millennium** — Tokyo QR postings exist, including Deep Learning QR (PhD physics/CS/math listed).
- **Goldman Sachs** Strats / algo R&D (moved **Toranomon Hills**, Jun 2024; ~800 Japan staff). Physics PhD → Associate Strats is a known path. Internal English; Japan-client desks want Japanese.
- **Morgan Stanley MUFG** — similar, more bilingual.

**Not Tokyo QR:**
- Jane Street → HK/SG
- Jump Trading Tokyo → data-center infra; research is SG/HK/Shanghai
- Two Sigma Tokyo → ~7 people, investor relations
- WorldQuant research → Seoul
- Citadel QR campus → HK/SG (Tokyo office is trading/eng)

Finance knowledge is **not** required at PhD entry. Interview: probability, stats, coding, thesis-to-non-physicist.

### Instruments / chips (physics PhD, wrong physics)

Thermo Fisher Tokyo NanoPort (TEM/SEM), Bruker, Keysight (Hachioji), Applied Materials / KLA / Lam (Tokyo–Yokohama), Apple Yokohama Tsunashima (optics). Japanese almost always required for FAE. NVIDIA Japan is SA/DevRel (RIKEN partnership), not a research lab.

## HSP (wage is the lever, not the gate)

PhD **30** + UTokyo **10** + papers **20** + age **10–15** ≈ **70–75** before income. Academia already clears HSP. Industry **accelerates PR** only above ~¥8–10M.

Income points (under 30; 30–34 loses the ¥4M band):

| Salary | Income pts |
|---|---|
| RIKEN postdoc ~¥4.5M | 10 if <30, else 0 — 1-year PR **tight** |
| SPDR / IBM ~¥6.6–8.1M | 20–30 — **80** if <30 is comfortable |
| Amazon AS / GS ≥¥10M | **40** (cap) — 80 automatic |
| J-Skip | needs **¥20M** |

1号ロ/ハ have a ¥3M income floor; 1号イ (research) does not. Official table: [ISA points](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/newimmiact_3_evaluate_index.html).

## Optionality during PhD (do not retarget DONUTS)

1. Keep the thesis a **physics** paper. Add one transferable artifact: methods / ML-for-PID with a public code path.
2. One internship probe D2–D3: IBM Research Tokyo or MSR Asia Tokyo.
3. Japanese to N3/N2 still multiplies living + HSP language points. English already clears US-lab / QR research desks.
4. Do **not** start LeetCode or quant prep now. GRE + thesis + Special Selection remain the gate ([[UTokyo_RIKEN]]).
