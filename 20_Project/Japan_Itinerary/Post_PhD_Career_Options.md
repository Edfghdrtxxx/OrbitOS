Reading these rows:
- The UTokyo Physics figure is the closest match to this PhD. Its 42% counts postdocs as academic posts, so it measures the first job after the degree, not a permanent post.
- The NISTEP figure shows the queue after that first job: two thirds of postdocs are still postdocs a year later, at an average age of 38.
- No row is specific to experimental nuclear physics in 2030. Together they show that permanent research posts go to a minority and that half of UTokyo Physics doctoral completers leave for industry or government at graduation. They do not show that no posts exist.

## Evidence gaps

Each item would move the research-versus-industry decision.

1. Where DONUTS and CNS doctoral graduates of the last ten years work now. The CNS lab page and the DONUTS site list no alumni, so the source is Imai or senior students. He may raise it at a later meeting with Imai.
2. How many postdocs reach a post with no fixed term, by field. NISTEP RM-337 holds the table; only its headline figures were retrieved.
3. Permanent experimental nuclear physics openings per year on JREC-IN and at RIKEN.
4. Language and foreign-national hiring practice in the accelerator divisions of SHI and Toshiba.
5. PhD hires per year into Tokyo and Singapore quantitative research seats.
6. Employer visa-sponsorship practice for new PhDs in each candidate country.
