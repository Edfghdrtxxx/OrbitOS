The Counter-Strike 2 landscape remains top-heavy. Team Vitality, led by the peerless ZywOo, sit clearly atop the world rankings with a commanding points gap to the field. A chasing pack of Spirit (donk), NaVi, Falcons (m0NESY / NiKo) and a transitioning MOUZ jostle for the second tier, while the current meta rewards structured, utility-heavy default setups and strong AWP-anchored CT sides. Star firepower and map-pool adaptation are decisive, and Swiss-stage bracket positioning keeps the mid-table volatile.

Major Context LIVE EVENT

Most recent completed CS2 Major: StarLadder Budapest Major 2025 (Budapest, Hungary; Nov 24 – Dec 14, 2025). Winner: Team Vitality, defeating FaZe Clan 3–1 in the first-ever best-of-five Major grand final — making Vitality the first team to win back-to-back Majors since Astralis in 2019. Currently ongoing: the IEM Cologne Major 2026 (Cologne, Germany; $1.25M, 32 teams, June 2–21). Playoffs begin June 18 and the champion is crowned June 21 — not yet decided as of June 14. Vitality entered as heavy favorites.
