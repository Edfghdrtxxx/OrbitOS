# Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics Drill Sets (Sets 17–20) · 10% ETS Weight

## Topic Overview
10% of the exam. High yield items: ideal gas processes on $PV$ diagrams, Carnot and generic engine efficiencies, equipartition theorem, entropy changes, and Boltzmann distribution calculations.

## Problem Set Index

### Set 17: Laws of Thermodynamics & State Equations (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Zeroth and First laws, ideal gas law, equations of state (van der Waals), internal energy $U(T)$, work done in isobaric/isochoric/isothermal/adiabatic paths.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 4 Practice Set 1.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 18: Heat Engines, Cycles & Entropy (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Second law of thermodynamics, heat engines and refrigerators, Carnot cycle and efficiency, Otto and Stirling cycles, entropy calculations for reversible and irreversible processes.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 4 Practice Set 2.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 19: Kinetic Theory & Equipartition (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Kinetic theory of gases, root-mean-square speed, Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution, equipartition theorem (degrees of freedom for mono/di/polyatomic gases), heat capacities $C_V$ and $C_P$.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 4 Practice Set 3.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 20: Statistical Ensembles & Quantum Statistics (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Microcanonical, canonical, and grand canonical ensembles, partition function $Z$, Boltzmann factor, Fermi-Dirac and Bose-Einstein distributions, blackbody radiation (Planck, Stefan-Boltzmann, Wien).
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 4 Practice Set 4.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`
