# Optics & Waves Drill Sets (Sets 14–16) · 8% ETS Weight (~6 Qs on 70Q exam)

## Topic Overview
Optics and wave phenomena cover 8% of the test (~6 questions on the 70-question computer exam). Major high-yield topics include interference conditions, diffraction minima/maxima, thin-film phase changes, and Doppler shifts.
