# Atomic Physics & Special Relativity Drill Sets (Sets 26–30) · 16% ETS Weight (~11 Qs on 70Q exam)

## Topic Overview
Atomic Physics (10%) and Special Relativity (6%) combine for 16% of the exam (~11 questions on the 70-question computer exam). Major areas: Bohr model transitions, fine structure, selection rules, Zeeman effect, Lorentz factor $\gamma$, relativistic energy-momentum invariant $E^2 - p^2 c^2 = m^2 c^4$, and 4-vectors.

## Problem Set Index

### Set 26: Atomic Structure & Early Quantum (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Photoelectric effect, Compton scattering, de Broglie wavelength, Bohr atom, Rydberg formula, Moseley's law, Franck-Hertz experiment.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 5 (§5.7 Atomic) + Prep Studio Bank + ETS Drill Items.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 27: Spin-Orbit, Fine Structure & Zeeman Effect (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Electron configuration, Hund's rules, spectroscopic notation $^{2S+1}L_J$, spin-orbit coupling, fine and hyperfine splitting, normal and anomalous Zeeman effect, selection rules ($\Delta l = \pm 1, \Delta m = 0, \pm 1$).
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 5 (§5.7 Spin-Orbit & Fine Structure) + Prep Studio Bank.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 28: Relativistic Kinematics & Space-Time (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Postulates of special relativity, Lorentz transformation matrix, time dilation, length contraction, relativity of simultaneity, relativistic velocity addition formula.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 6 (Special Relativity) + Prep Studio Bank.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 29: Relativistic Dynamics & 4-Vectors (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Relativistic momentum $p = \gamma m v$, total energy $E = \gamma m c^2$, kinetic energy $K = (\gamma - 1)mc^2$, invariant mass $E^2 - p^2 c^2 = m^2 c^4$, threshold energy for particle creation, photon collisions.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 6 (Relativistic Energy & 4-Vectors) + Prep Studio Bank.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 30: Nuclear Properties, Decays & Reactions (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Nuclear radii $R \approx R_0 A^{1/3}$, binding energy curve and semi-empirical mass formula (liquid drop model), radioactive decay law ($N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$, half-life), alpha/beta/gamma decays, nuclear fission and fusion energetics.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 8 (§8.1 Nuclear Physics) + Prep Studio Bank.
