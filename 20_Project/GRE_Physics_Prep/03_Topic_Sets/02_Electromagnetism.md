# Electromagnetism Drill Sets (Sets 08–13) · 18% ETS Weight

## Topic Overview
Electromagnetism forms 18% of the test. High-frequency patterns: symmetry in Gauss's/Ampère's laws, boundary conditions, image charges, and LC/RL circuit transients.

## Problem Set Index

### Set 08: Electrostatics & Field Potentials (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Coulomb's law, electric field calculations, electric potential, Gauss's law for spherical/cylindrical/planar symmetries, conductors in electrostatic equilibrium.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 1.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 09: Capacitors, Dielectrics & DC Circuits (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Capacitance formulas, electrostatic energy density, dielectrics and bound charge, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's loop/junction rules, RC circuit transients.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 2.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 10: Magnetostatics & Ampère's Law (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Biot-Savart law, Ampère's law, magnetic fields of wires/coils/solenoids/sheets, Lorentz force, cyclotron motion, magnetic dipoles and torque.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 3.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 11: Induction, Inductance & RL/LC Circuits (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Faraday's law of induction, Lenz's law, motional EMF, mutual and self-inductance, RL and LC oscillations, energy in magnetic fields.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 4.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 12: Maxwell's Equations & Electromagnetic Waves (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** Displacement current, Maxwell's four equations, plane EM waves in vacuum, speed of light, Poynting vector, radiation pressure and momentum.
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 5.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`

### Set 13: Fields in Matter, Boundary Conditions & AC Circuits (25 Qs)
- **Subtopics:** $\mathbf{D}$ and $\mathbf{H}$ fields, polarization and magnetization, EM boundary conditions at interfaces, AC circuits (phasors, impedance, resonance).
- **Source:** Kahn Ch. 2 Practice Set 6 + Mixed EM Review.
- **Log / Status:** `[ ] Open`
