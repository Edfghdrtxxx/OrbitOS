# TOEFL iBT Listen-and-Repeat — 7-Day Core Method

## Question & Brief
**QUESTION:** For a student one week out from the redesigned TOEFL iBT "Listen and Repeat" task (7 sentences, played once, no text, beep-then-record, ~8-12s window, scored 0-5 on content accuracy + pronunciation + fluency/prosody), whose dominant failure is working-memory encoding (understands the words but can't hold a 12-16-word sentence to the end) with a secondary decoding-speed issue, and whose collapse point is ~11-13 words — what is the single most effective core method to train in 7 days: (A) meaning-anchored chunking, (B) acoustic shadowing, or (C) both sequenced? What is the optimal daily protocol given 45-60 min/day?

**CONTEXT:** Wall at 11-13 words → sentences 5-7 are the battleground. Zero prep time on test. Scoring tolerates minor function-word slips (Score 4) but penalizes hesitation/restart. Calibration tool: PrepEx AI. One-week horizon means neurological decoding-speed gains are unrealistic; technique/strategy gains are realistic.

## Stage 1 — First Opinions
### Member-Host — Chunking core + shadow warm-up; lever is killing subvocal rehearsal
Method C. Reframes mechanism: beginning evaporates because user subvocally re-says early words during listening, jamming the phonological loop. Chunking frees the loop by storing ~3 semantic pointers instead of ~13 phonemes. Shadowing can't be core (echo caps ~2s / 7±2 items past the 13-word wall) but is best warm-up for the secondary decoding issue. [NOTE: this causal mechanism was later found flawed — see Stage 2/3.]

### Member-Grok — (C) sequenced: chunking core, shadowing only as 10-min ear warm-up
Failure is encoding not hearing; user already parses in real time, so missing skill is committing meaning instead of a word list. 7 days installs a 3-chunk habit, won't myelinate decoding. Distinctive rule: if the opening is gone, skip it and finish — never freeze/restart. Retry same item from chunk-memory, not a second listen. Success metric: day 7, 13-16 word items hold first clause without pause, PrepEx content ≥4.

### Member-Gemini — (A) Pure chunking; shadowing is a biological dead-end
Drop shadowing entirely as a memory tool. Echoic trace degrades in 2-4s, cannot rehearse during incoming 4-5s speech without destructive interference → hard cliff past 13 words. Dual-register: hold early clauses semantically, let the tail clause ride raw acoustic buffer. Compression calisthenics: repeat back gist in 5-8 words to kill phonological anxiety. Full-simulation runs chasing fluency over lexical precision.

### Final Answer
Core method: meaning-anchored chunking, with a strictly-bounded 10-min acoustic-shadowing warm-up (Grok's Method C). All three converge that chunking is the only mechanism surviving the 11-13-word wall in 7 days; the raw-acoustic buffer decays in 2-4s and cannot hold sentences 5-7. Shadowing stays (user reported a secondary decoding deficit — dropping it ignores a named problem) but capped at 10 min, ear-warmup only, never the memory strategy; Gemini's interference warning is mitigated by the cap and clip-based format.

Non-negotiable council-validated techniques:
- Dual-register (Gemini): hold EARLY clauses as compressed meaning; let the FINAL clause ride the raw acoustic buffer (still fresh at beep). Exploits primacy (scaffold) + recency (buffer).
- Never freeze (Grok): if the opening is gone, speak what you have and finish in one pass. Hesitation/restart scored harder than a dropped function word.

Correction: target is NOT loose gist. Content-word accuracy caps band 5 near-verbatim; content-word swaps score 3. Chunk to regenerate the ACTUAL words. Only function words (a/the) are safe casualties.

### Unresolved Tension
Shadowing's role: patches the secondary decoding issue but risks reinforcing verbatim streaming that fights chunking — mitigated by the 10-min cap, not eliminated.

### Recommendation
50-min daily: 10 min shadowing warm-up → 30 min chunking core on 12-18-word sentences (dual-register, never-freeze, content-word error log) → 10 min full 7-sentence timed mock. Score on PrepEx AI at day 3 and day 6 to verify transfer.
