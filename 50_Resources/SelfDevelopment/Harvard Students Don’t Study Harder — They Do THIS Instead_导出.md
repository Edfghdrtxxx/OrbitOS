  - Close your notes, take a blank sheet of paper, and write everything you remember about the topic. Then compare it with your notes to identify gaps and review only what you missed.


### Terminology
- **Forgetting Curve**: A memory concept showing how quickly people forget new information when they do not review it.
- **Spaced Repetition**: A study method where information is reviewed at planned intervals to improve long-term retention.
- **Pomodoro Technique**: A focus method that uses 25-minute study sessions followed by short 5-minute breaks.
- **Active Recall**: A learning technique that strengthens memory by forcing the brain to retrieve information without looking at notes.
- **Desirable Difficulty**: The idea that making learning slightly challenging improves memory and understanding over time.
