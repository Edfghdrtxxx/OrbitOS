# Claude Account Suspension Appeals

## Core idea

Present a factual account of your normal work and the activity surrounding the suspension, then request human review, an explanation of the relevant action and rule, and restoration if the decision was mistaken. Describe actual products, projects, and workflows; explain a potentially misunderstood integration only when it is relevant and true. Describe the effect on your work, study, or access to important project context without exaggeration. Use your own dates and evidence rather than copying the author's examples or declarations. [Source: AI超元域's appeal guide](https://x.com/aisuperdomain/status/2105660384350744770).

## Procedure recommended by the post

1. Submit the appeal form with your typical use and the events surrounding the suspension.
2. Email `privacy@anthropic.com` before the form receives a final decision.
3. Use the Google backup form optionally, with the same factual account.

The author reports that the privacy address referred their inquiry to a human, while the support and safety addresses returned instructions to use the form. This is one person's experience; it does not establish that the privacy route improves the chance of reinstatement. The post itself questions the backup form's usefulness. [Source: the post](https://x.com/aisuperdomain/status/2105660384350744770).

## Appeal preparation

1. Identify the suspended account, suspension date, and reference number.
2. Describe the products, real projects, and ordinary workflow associated with that account.
3. Give a dated account of the last successful use and discovery of the suspension.
4. Include relevant ownership, payment, refund, and previous-appeal evidence.
5. Request human review and the specific reason for the decision.
6. Keep explanations consistent across the form and email.

Do not present a suspected cause as an established fact, copy another person's projects or denials, or claim a refund is outstanding when a refund notice exists. Recheck these destinations before a future appeal because forms and support procedures can change.
