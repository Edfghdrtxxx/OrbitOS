Classical mechanics · projectile + Hooke

Spring-launched ball returning to launch height

A ball of mass $m$ is launched at $45^\circ$ from the horizontal by a spring of spring constant $k$ depressed by a displacement $d$. What horizontal distance $x$ does the ball travel before returning to its height at launch?

spring $k$, compressed by $d$ ball mass $m$ free-flight parabola horizontal range $x$

Energy → launch speed

Ideal release: stored spring energy becomes kinetic energy of the ball.

$$\tfrac12 k d^2 = \tfrac12 m v^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad v = d\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$

Projectile range at $45^\circ$

Launch at height $y_0$, return to $y_0$:

$$x = \frac{v^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$$

With $\theta=45^\circ$, $\sin 90^\circ=1$, so $x=v^2/g$.

Horizontal distance

$$x = \dfrac{k d^{2}}{m g}$$

Substitute $v=d\sqrt{k/m}$ into $x=v^2/g$: $x = k d^2/(m g)$. At $45^\circ$ this is the level-return range (and the maximum over angle).
