# TOEFL iBT 2026 — Project Context

Shared context for agents in this project. The TOEFL was redesigned effective **January 21, 2026**; do NOT generate materials in the pre-2026 format (independent/integrated essays, 4 long speaking tasks, long reading sets — all retired). Facts below are from ETS primary sources; items marked *(memory)* are Reid's personal context, and items marked *(derived)* are inferences from ETS data.

## Structure (ETS Test Overview PDF; ETS test-content page)

Fixed order **Reading → Listening → Writing → Speaking**, under 2 hours total, no breaks, at test centers or Home Edition. Reading and Listening are **multistage adaptive**: stage-1 performance selects stage-2 content.

| Section | Task types | Items | Base time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading | Complete the Words · Read in Daily Life · Read an Academic Passage | 50 | 30 min |
| Listening | Listen and Choose a Response · Conversation · Announcement · Academic Talk | 47 | 29 min |
| Writing | Build a Sentence · Write an Email · Write for an Academic Discussion | 12 | 23 min |
| Speaking | Listen and Repeat · Take an Interview | 11 | 8 min |

**Reading.** Complete the Words: academic paragraph of 70–100 words, first sentence intact, 10 words with missing letters (ETS describes "second half of every second word" deleted; real samples show variable-length gaps — follow samples). Read in Daily Life: 15–150-word everyday texts (menus, notices, texts), 2–3 MCQs each. Academic Passage: ~200 words, 5 questions.

**Listening.** Choose a Response: a heard (not written) question/statement → pick the appropriate reply. Conversation: 2 questions. Announcement: 40–85 words. Academic Talk: 100–250 words, 4 questions, semi-spontaneous lecture style. Accents: North American, British, Australian.

**Writing.** Build a Sentence: reorder words into a grammatical reply. Write an Email: 7 min, scenario-based. Academic Discussion: 10 min, forum post; ETS says an effective response has ≥100 words.

**Speaking.** Listen and Repeat: 7 sentences of increasing length, exact repetition, 8–12 s each. Take an Interview: simulated interviewer, 4 questions, 45 s per answer; ETS materials describe no separate prep time — answers follow the question directly. The rubric rewards conversational pace and elaboration; a memorization penalty is prep-source convention, not stated in the ETS rubric.

## Scoring (ETS Test Overview PDF; ETS score-scale pages)

- Each section and the overall score are **bands 1.0–6.0 in 0.5 steps**; overall = average of the four sections rounded to the nearest half band (ETS example: 5.125 → 5.0).
- Individual Speaking/Writing tasks are rated 0–5 by rubric; section raw scores (R 0–30, L 0–30, W 0–20, S 0–55) convert to bands.
- CEFR mapping: 6 = C2, 5–5.5 = C1, 4–4.5 = B2, 3–3.5 = B1, 2–2.5 = A2, 1–1.5 = A1.
- Legacy 0–120 overall reported alongside bands during a 2-year transition. Concordance (overall): 4.0 ≈ 72+, 4.5 ≈ 86+, **5.0 ≈ 95+**, 5.5 ≈ 107+, 6.0 = 114+.
- MyBest scores (best sections within 2 years) included; scores in ~3 days.

## Reid's prep context *(memory)*

- Target: 100+ on the legacy overall scale — ≈ band 5.0–5.5 *(derived from ETS concordance)* — with Speaking 5.0/6.0. Deadline: **late August 2026**.
- Priorities: Writing and Speaking. Speaking uses a four-beat structure (choice → reason → detail → takeaway) with reusable syntactic frames only (exact reuse risks the memorization penalty); speaking rate needs to rise toward 120–150 wpm. Delivery (pronunciation/fluency) is strong; lift comes from grammar accuracy and natural phrasing under time pressure.
- Writing weak spot: Build a Sentence accuracy; Academic Discussion needs fuller *why* development, tighter language control (spelling, prepositions), and stronger closings without filler. Email is near-target with light proofreading.
- Listening weak spot: unfamiliar non-physics academic vocabulary (biology, psychology, art history) triggers cascading focus loss; address format familiarity first. Avoid skipping items when unsure.
- On a 10-week study plan; the ETS official free practice test is reserved for the final weeks.
- Working style: verify format claims against ETS before generating practice material; flag test-prep-sourced claims as such; keep materials concise.
